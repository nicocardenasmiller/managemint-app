# Managemint wording
#
# Every piece of text people see in Managemint lives in this file: the website, the setup page, the web app
# (laptop and iPhone) and the Android app. Change the text after the colon and leave the name before it alone.
#
# How a line works:
#   desktop.topbar.today: Today
#   ^ name (don't change)  ^ text (change this)
#
# - Words in curly braces, like {name} or {n}, are filled in by the app (a project name, a number). Keep them,
#   but you can move them around in the sentence.
# - Some lines come in pairs ending in _one and _other: the first is used for 1 ("1 task"), the second for more.
# - A few lines are lists split by " | " or by spaces (month names, weekday names). Keep the same number of items.
# - Website and setup-page lines can contain HTML such as <b>bold</b>, <em>italic</em> or <a href="...">links</a>.
#   {{REPO}} becomes the public GitHub repository's name.
# - A line that starts with a space after the colon keeps that space (for example " · undone").
# - Lines starting with # are notes and are ignored. See "How to edit wording" in README.md for how to publish.
#
# After editing, `node copy/check.mjs` (also run by the backend and desktop tests) checks that every name the app
# uses is here and that nothing here is unused.

## privacy
site.privacy.page_title: Privacy · Managemint
site.privacy.nav_start: Get started
site.privacy.nav_app: Get the app
site.privacy.title: Privacy
site.privacy.updated: Last updated November 22, 2026.
site.privacy.intro: Managemint is a small test project by Nico, shared with friends. There is no company and no central server. Each person runs their own copy in their own accounts, so <strong>Nico can't see your tasks, your calendar or your AI key</strong>.
site.privacy.storage_title: Where your data is stored
site.privacy.storage: Your tasks, projects, notes and settings are stored in a database in <strong>your own Cloudflare account</strong>, created when you deploy. The phone app keeps a copy on your phone so it works offline. The web app keeps a sign-in key in your browser. You can delete everything by deleting the Worker and its database in your Cloudflare dashboard.
site.privacy.services_title: Services your server uses
site.privacy.services_cloudflare: <strong>Cloudflare</strong> runs your server and stores your database. Cloudflare's own <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/">privacy policy</a> applies.
site.privacy.services_anthropic: <strong>Anthropic</strong> (the AI) receives the text of what you capture, your answers when you set up a project with the assistant, and your planning notes, so it can turn them into tasks and dates. It's your own API key and your own account. Anthropic doesn't train on API data by default. See <a href="https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy">Anthropic's privacy policy</a>.
site.privacy.services_calendar: <strong>Your calendar app</strong>, if you subscribe to your calendar link. Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook downloads your dated tasks (titles, notes, dates and project names) from your server through a private link. Anyone who has that link can read those tasks, so keep it to yourself; you can replace it with a new one on your setup page at any time.
site.privacy.services_google: <strong>Google Calendar direct sync</strong>, only on servers that have it set up. Managemint asks only for permission to manage calendars it creates itself. It creates one calendar called "Managemint" and can't see or change your other calendars or your email. You can disconnect on your setup page or in your <a href="https://myaccount.google.com/permissions">Google account settings</a>.
site.privacy.services_github: <strong>GitHub</strong>: your server checks the public Managemint repository for new versions. That request contains no personal data.
site.privacy.voice_title: Voice input
site.privacy.voice: When you speak a task, your phone or browser turns the speech into text with its own speech recognition (Google on Android, Apple in Safari, Google in Chrome). Managemint only receives the text.
site.privacy.google_app_title: The Google sign-in app
site.privacy.google_app: The calendar link needs no sign-in. Google Calendar direct sync connections go through a Google app registered by Nico. The sign-in returns to a page on this website, which forwards you straight back to your own server. That page stores nothing. Your calendar token is saved only on your server. Google shows Nico the number of people who have connected, not who they are or what is in their calendars.
site.privacy.website_title: This website
site.privacy.website: This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics, no cookies and no third-party scripts or fonts. If you type your server's address into "Open your server", it is saved only in your browser. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under <a href="https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement">its privacy statement</a>.
site.privacy.feedback_title: Feedback
site.privacy.feedback: Bug reports and suggestions are GitHub issues, which are public. Don't include task contents or anything private in them.
site.privacy.questions_title: Questions
site.privacy.questions: Ask Nico, or <a href="https://github.com/{{REPO}}/issues/new">open an issue</a>.
site.privacy.footer_made_by: Made by Nico, a PhD student in political science.
site.privacy.footer_links: <a href="./">Home</a> · <a href="https://github.com/{{REPO}}">Source code</a> (MIT licence)
